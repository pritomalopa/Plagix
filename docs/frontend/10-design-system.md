## Brand Identity & Visual Direction

PLAGIX is designed as a professional, modern academic SaaS utility. The visual tone inspires trust, clarity, and precision. Avoid playful or cartoonish elements.

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Deep Academic Navy (`#1E3A8A` / Tailwind `blue-900`)
- **Secondary:** Slate Blue (`#3B82F6` / Tailwind `blue-500`)
- **Neutral:** Clean slate grays (`slate-50` to `slate-900`)
- **Semantic Colors:**
  - Success / Relevant: Emerald (`#10B981`)
  - Warning / Moderate: Amber (`#F59E0B`)
  - Destructive / High Risk: Rose (`#F43F5E`)
  - Info / Neutral: Blue (`#3B82F6`)

### Typography
- **Font Family:** Inter or Geist Sans (sans-serif system stack)
- **Headings:** Bold, clear hierarchy (Text-2xl for page titles, Text-lg for section headers)
- **Body:** Regular 14px/16px with optimal line height (1.5)
