# Reports and Export UI

**PLAGIX v1.0**  
**Last updated:** 21 November 2026

---

## Report Formats

1. **Batch Summary PDF:** Class-level statistics, score distribution charts, relevance breakdown, and high-similarity pairs list.
2. **Gradebook CSV:** Tabular export compatible with Excel, Google Sheets, and external LMS systems.
3. **Individual Student Report ZIP:** Individual PDF reports for each student containing relevance evidence, rubric criterion breakdown, matched peer passages, and teacher comments.

---

## Mandatory Disclaimers

All exported reports include the required integrity disclaimer:
> *"Similarity is an indicator of content overlap and requires human review; it is not automatic proof of plagiarism or academic misconduct."*
