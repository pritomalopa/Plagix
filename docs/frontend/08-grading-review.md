# Grading, Teacher Review, and Appeals

**PLAGIX v1.0**  
**Last updated:** 21 November 2026

---

## Grading Policy & Safeguards

1. **Rubric-Based Scoring:** Criterion-by-criterion breakdown with suggested points and rationale when a rubric is provided.
2. **Relevance-Only Fallback:** If no rubric is provided, system calculates relevance-based provisional marks with a clear warning that content correctness is not fully verified.
3. **Integrity Separation:** Similarity and AI-writing indicators are kept strictly separate from content grades. They never automatically deduct marks or accuse students.
4. **Teacher Authority:** The teacher retains final approval authority over all marks.

---

## Teacher Override & Audit Trail

- **Manual Override:** Teachers can edit provisional marks, dismiss similarity flags, and add custom notes.
- **Mandatory Reason:** Any grade override requires entering a reason.
- **Audit Logging:** Every review event, mark change, flag dismissal, and approval timestamp is recorded immutably in `audit_logs`.
